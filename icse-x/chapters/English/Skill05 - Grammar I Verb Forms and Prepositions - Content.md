## 1. Complete Lesson

### Part (i): verb forms in a passage

The passage is usually a short **narrative in the past tense**, with eight blanks. For each blank you write the **correct form of the verb in brackets** — often with an auxiliary (*had*, *was*, *could*, *will*) or a negative.

**How to answer:**

1. **Read the whole passage first**, to see its main tense (usually the simple past).
2. For each blank, ask: **when** did this happen compared with the main events? Is it **continuing**, **completed earlier**, **habitual** or **in direct speech**?
3. Check the **word before and after** the blank: a preposition (*on ___*) needs a **gerund** (*-ing*); *started ___* takes *-ing* or *to + verb*; a subject like *he* needs a singular verb.
4. Write only the **number and the verb form**, in order — do not copy the passage.

### The tense map

| Situation | Form | Example (from specimens) |
|---|---|---|
| main events in a past narrative | simple past | Under a tree I **found** a dry spot. (2027) |
| an action in progress at a past moment | past continuous | the fan **was not working** (2025); the deer **was warning** the jungle folk (2027) |
| an action completed before another past action | past perfect | she **had used** up all her water (2025); He **had travelled** far … since that day (2027); Nag and Nagaina **had disappeared** already (2024) |
| a general truth or habit inside a narrative | simple present | When a snake misses its stroke, it never **says** anything (2024) |
| direct speech about now | simple present / present continuous | "I **need** a bottle of cold water" (2025); "What **are you doing**?" (2025) |
| direct speech about the future | future | "You **will never see** your money again." (2025) |
| ability in the past | could | he **could** manage two snakes (2024); A fellow passenger **could not believe** … (2025) |
| after a preposition | gerund (-ing) | on **catching** my eye (2027) |
| after *start*, *begin*, *keep* | -ing (or *to* + verb) | a deer started **calling** (2027) |
| participle phrase | -ing / -ed | Robin **stretched** out at my feet (2027); looked all round him, **chattering** with rage (2024) |

### Common traps in part (i)

- **Negative verbs:** "(not work)" → *was not working* / *did not work* — keep the *not* in the right place.
- **"(cannot believe)"** in a past narrative → *could not believe*.
- **"(you do)"** in a question → *are you doing* — question word order.
- **Subject–verb agreement:** "it never ___ (say)" → *says*.
- **Earlier past:** look for *already*, *since*, *by then*, *before* → past perfect.
- **Both answers may be accepted** when the meaning allows it (2025: *halted / would halt*).

### Part (ii): prepositions and phrasal verbs

Each sentence has one blank for **one word**. Most answers are:

1. **Fixed prepositions** after verbs, adjectives and nouns — *comply **with***, *dispose **of***, *impress **upon***, *prefer death **to** dishonour*, *quarrel **over** the price*, *advise **against***.
2. **Particles of phrasal verbs** — *cut **across***, *turn **down***, *bring **out***, *put **off***, *back **out***, *get **along***, *look **after***, *taken **aback***.
3. **Time and place** — *since* (a point of time) / *for* (a period); *at* a desk; *by* appearances.

### Prepositions of time

| Use | Preposition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a point in time (clock) | at | at 6 o'clock |
| days and dates | on | on Monday; on 5th June |
| months, years, parts of the day | in | in May; in 2027; in the morning |
| a starting point | since | since 2019; ever since the accident |
| a length of time | for | for two years; for the past two years |
| before a deadline | by | by Friday |
| throughout a period | during | during the holidays |

### Prepositions of place and movement

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at | a point | at the desk; at the gate |
| in | inside | in the room |
| on | a surface | on the table |
| across | from one side to the other | cut across the field |
| through | inside, from one end to the other | through the tunnel |
| up / down | higher / lower | climb up the hill |
| along | following a line | walk along the river |
| over | above; covering | jump over the wall |

| Verb + preposition | Example |
|---|---|

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, students will be able to:

1. **Identify** the main tense of a narrative passage and choose verb forms that fit.
2. **Use** the past continuous, past perfect, present (for truths and direct speech), future, modals, gerunds and participles correctly in context.
3. **Form** negatives and questions correctly from a bracketed verb.
4. **Choose** fixed prepositions after verbs, adjectives and nouns.
5. **Complete** common phrasal verbs with the right particle.
6. **Use** prepositions of time and place accurately.

---

## 4. Vocabulary (Key Terms)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tense | the form of a verb that shows time |
| auxiliary verb | a helping verb (be, have, do, will, can, could) |
| past perfect | had + past participle: an action before another past action |
| continuous (progressive) | be + -ing: an action in progress |
| gerund | the -ing form used as a noun (on catching my eye) |
| participle | a verb form used as an adjective or in a phrase (-ing / -ed) |
| modal | can, could, may, might, will, would, shall, should, must |
| preposition | a word showing relation of place, time, etc. (in, on, at) |
| phrasal verb | a verb + particle with a new meaning (put off = postpone) |
| particle | the adverb or preposition in a phrasal verb (off, up, out) |

### 8.1 Verb forms — the specimen passages solved

**ICSE 2027 specimen, Q5(i)** — a jungle passage (adapted from Jim Corbett):

*The sun (0) was near rising… Under a nearby tree I (1) ___ (find) a dry spot … and with Robin (2) ___ (stretch) out at my feet, when suddenly a deer started (3) ___ (call)… on (4) ___ (catch) my eye … He (5) ___ (travel) far along the road of experience since that day he had first (6) ___ (hear) the alarm call of a langur and he (7) ___ (know) now … that the deer (8) ___ (warn) the jungle folk of the presence of a leopard.*

| Blank | Answer (CISCE scheme) | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | found | simple past — a main event |
| 2 | stretched | past participle in the phrase "with Robin stretched out" |
| 3 | calling | "started" + -ing |
| 4 | catching | after the preposition "on" — gerund |
| 5 | had travelled | earlier past, with "since that day" |
| 6 | heard | past perfect already shown by "had first"; past participle |
| 7 | knew | simple past — the narrative's main tense |
| 8 | was warning | an action in progress at that moment |

**ICSE 2025 specimen, Q5(i)** — a railway passage:

| Blank | Bracket | Answer (CISCE scheme) | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (not work) | was not working | a continuing state at that moment |
| 2 | (use) | had used | completed before the moment described |
| 3 | (halt) | halted / would halt | both accepted — the train stopped / was due to stop |
| 4 | (need) | need | direct speech, present |
| 5 | (disappear) | disappeared | simple past |
| 6 | (cannot believe) | could not believe | past form of "cannot" |
| 7 | (you do) | are you doing | direct question, present continuous |
| 8 | (never see) | will never see | direct speech, future |

| Inside quotation marks? | the tense the speaker would use (often present or future) |
| After a preposition (on, by, without)? | -ing |
| After did not / could / will? | base form |

### Prepositions — remember

- **Time:** at 6 o'clock · on Monday · in May / 2027 · since a point · for a period · by a deadline.
- **Fixed:** prefer to · dispose of · comply with · impress upon · advise against · quarrel over · go by · speak to · depend on · insist on · suffer from.
- **Phrasal verbs:** cut across · turn down · put off · back out · bring out · get along · look after · taken aback · call off · put up with · take after · look up to.
